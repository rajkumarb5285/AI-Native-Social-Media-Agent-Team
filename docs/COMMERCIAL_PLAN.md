# Commercial pilot plan

## Initial offer
“Social Content Operations Copilot for small businesses and marketing agencies.” Deliver researched campaign briefs (when research integration is connected), platform-specific drafts, approval workflows, and a weekly report. Avoid promising fully autonomous posting until official platform integrations are live.

## Pilot
1. Interview 5–10 marketing agencies or local brands about their workflow and supported platforms.
2. Pick one narrow customer segment and one platform.
3. Run a 2–4 week paid pilot with human approvals.
4. Measure hours saved, draft approval rate, revision count, on-time publishing, and attributable leads.
5. Price after measuring API/model cost and support time. Use a setup fee plus monthly subscription only when recurring value is demonstrated.
6. Publish a clear list of supported integrations, limits, data handling, and service expectations.

## Do not promise
- Guaranteed viral growth or revenue.
- A specific count of autonomous agents unless independently implemented and tested.
- Live analytics, competitor research, or publishing without working integrations.
- “Production-ready” security before authentication, tenant isolation, token handling, and independent review are complete.
